# CashLoom settlement handoff — TLDR

CashLoom can use self-certifying keys and portable files without a hosted CashLoom or corporate account. Cambridge TCG currently lets a member declare an optional key fingerprint and lets a seller freeze one matched trade's exact GBP and fulfilment terms into a participant-only packet.

The declaration is not proof of key control or legal identity. The packet is not a Pay Link, payment, escrow, exchange-rate quote, chain receipt, shipping unlock, or payout instruction. It moves no money and changes no trade state.
