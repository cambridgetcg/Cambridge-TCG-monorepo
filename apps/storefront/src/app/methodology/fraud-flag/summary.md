# Fraud flag — TLDR

A matching rule creates a risk signal for operator review; it is not proof of fraud. Every unresolved medium-or-higher signal automatically subtracts 20 trust-score points on recalculation, which can change limits, commission, escrow/inspection routing, payout holds and the displayed tier. A signal does not clear itself: an authenticated operator must resolve or dismiss it with a written reason; the signal change and governance record commit together, and trust recalculation is awaited. Signal creation does not directly suspend an account or stop an already-earned payout; the separately logged emergency freeze is human-only.
