# PRISM Signals product flow — TLDR

**Synthetic preview · no live market data · no payment.** PRISM Signals by Cambridge TCG wraps the public opportunity-signal boundary in a branded web and Telegram reading: only coarse bands, evidence confidence, liquidity, risks, and fixed non-claims may cross. A separate signed-in management path can record revocable product-specific interest for at most 180 days only while intake is enabled, and remains available for owner status and withdrawal when intake is paused; it is not general marketing consent, an invitation, payment, queue position, or access. The extracted unpublished workspace core, lock-first durable runtime, and Stripe/Telegram Stars normalizers now test the next boundary, but no public provider webhook consumes them. Stripe, Telegram Stars, PayPal, and crypto remain off. No live offer, purchasable price, production engine, accepted payment, active paid entitlement, paid channel, or outbound delivery exists.
