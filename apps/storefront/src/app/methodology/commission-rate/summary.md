# Commission rate — TLDR

Commission has been 0% since 21 July 2026 — Cambridge TCG takes no commission, so sellers keep 100% of a sale. New P2P sales and auctions were retired on 23 September 2026. Trades and auctions agreed on or after 21 July 2026 carry 0% commission, including those still settling; earlier trades keep the commission recorded when they were matched. There are no tiers and no membership; the rate is the same for everyone. It is computed in one place (`computeCommissionAmount`), which returns 0 unconditionally.
