# RewardsPro: brand kit

> Cashback paid as Shopify store credit, plus VIP tiers, for Shopify stores. Free.

Generated by `artcraft-brand brandkit.py 1.2` with vectorcraft-cli 0.13.1, from `source/brand.json`. Every vector and raster was drawn by VectorCraft in one headless session (`brand.vectorcraft` is that document); stdlib Python did the colour maths, the ICO packing and these documents. Open `index.html` to review everything on light and dark grounds.

## Logo

**Mark:** from `source/mark-source.svg`, 2 shape(s) in #17BEBB, #1A3E5C, normalised by VectorCraft (transforms baked, viewBox offset removed) and rebuilt from its path data. **Wordmark:** "RewardsPro" in Inter Semi Bold, tracking -10/1000 em, outlined. Every SVG is outlined paths with no `<text>`, no strokes and named ids.

Proportions are derived from the mark, so they hold at any size:

- **Horizontal lockup:** wordmark cap height = 0.50 x mark height; mark-to-word gap = 0.30 x mark height. The wordmark is centred on the mark by its cap height (the band from baseline to the top of a capital H), not by its bounding box, so descenders and lowercase letters do not pull it off centre.
- **Stacked lockup:** cap height = 0.32 x mark height; gap from the mark to the top of the capitals = 0.21 x mark height; both centred on one axis.

**Clear space.** Keep a margin of at least **x** on every side of any logo, where **x is the wordmark's cap height** (the height of a capital H as set in the lockup). For the horizontal lockup x = 0.50 x the mark's height (48 px at the 1x size, 96 px tall). For the mark alone, use x = a quarter of the mark's height. Nothing else (text, edges, other logos) enters that space.

**Minimum sizes** (below these the wordmark loses its letterforms; use the mark alone):

| asset | screen | print |
|---|---|---|
| wordmark | 100 px wide (cap height 12 px) | 23 mm wide (cap height 3 mm) |
| horizontal lockup | 130 px wide (cap height 12 px) | 30 mm wide (cap height 3 mm) |
| stacked lockup | 100 px wide (cap height 12 px) | 23 mm wide (cap height 3 mm) |
| mark | 16 px (below 24 px use the hinted favicon cuts) | 6 mm |

**Colour versions.** Full colour on light grounds, where #17BEBB is 2.30:1 on #FFFFFF: `shape 2` reads there by its hue rather than by its edge. Where the edge must hold (very small sizes, one-ink print, low-contrast screens), draw #17BEBB as accent 500 #009A97 (3.46:1), the step that reaches 3:1 there, or use `mark-mono-black` or the wordmark. The `-reversed` lockups and `wordmark-reversed` are for dark grounds: the wordmark is white, and #1A3E5C moves to #658AAD (primary 500, 4.66:1 and APCA Lc -36.2 on #1B1D1F, the dark-mode UI colour). On dark grounds a mark colour moves up its own scale until it reaches 3:1 and APCA Lc 30 (the dark-mode UI bar). A badge that holds letters takes that same UI step: with its letters when they keep 4.5:1 on it, else with its letters cut out when the ground shows through them at 4.5:1, else with its letters in the ink that reads on it; only when none of these works does it take the step that keeps its letters at 4.5:1. White only when no step does. The one-colour marks are every shape in one ink.

**Do not:** recolour outside the palette, stretch, rotate, add effects or outlines, set the name in another font, rebuild the lockup by hand, or place the full-colour logo on a ground close to its own colours (a fill of the brand colour, a busy photo): use the one-colour or reversed versions there.

## Colour

Hex is the source value (sRGB). OKLCH is shown for reference and drives the scales; it is never written as a fill in any vector file. Contrast is WCAG 2.2 (the conformance bar); APCA Lc is in `colour/contrast.md` as guidance.

| colour | hex | oklch | rgb | on white | on black |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| primary | `#1A3E5C` | `oklch(35.3% 0.067 246.4)` | 26 62 92 | 11.13 | 1.89 |
| accent | `#17BEBB` | `oklch(72.6% 0.121 192.8)` | 23 190 187 | 2.30 | 9.12 |

Each colour has an 11-step OKLCH scale (50 to 950) in `colour/tokens.css`, `tokens.json` and the swatch libraries; the brand colour itself is the step nearest its lightness. brand.json names no neutral, so the neutral scale is derived from the primary's hue at low chroma; it has no brand colour of its own, and the roles use 50 #F6F7F7 (dark text), 100 #EAEBEC (light surface), 200 #D7D9DB (light border), 400 #9EA2A6 (dark text-muted), 600 #686C70 (light text-muted), 800 #404346 (dark border), 900 #2E3133 (dark surface), 950 #1B1D1F (light text, dark bg).

**Roles** (what the tokens are for). Each role is the step of its scale nearest the brand colour that reaches its WCAG 2.2 bar and an APCA floor: |Lc| 45 for text (`text-muted`, `*-text`) and 30 for UI (`*-ui`, `focus`), because WCAG 2 over-rates light colours on dark grounds. So on dark grounds the logo's lifted colour (#658AAD) is the dark-mode UI step: logo, focus rings and controls share one dark primary. In dark mode --primary is #658AAD (primary 500, 4.66:1 on the dark ground), not the brand colour #1A3E5C, which is 1.52:1 there and would disappear as a fill; light mode keeps it. `on-*` is the ink on a brand fill: the brand's dark text colour or white, whichever reads better at 4.5:1, the same ink the logo uses.

## Icons

- **App icons** (`apple-touch-icon`, `icon-192`, `icon-512`): full-bleed #FFFFFF tile, the mark in its own colours. The mark spans 69% of the tile (measured; icon.padding 0.18 a side), sized optically (sparse or wide marks a little larger, solid blocks a little smaller) and centred between its box centre and its centre of mass. No rounded corners are baked in: iOS and Android apply their own masks.
- **Maskable** (`icon-maskable-512`): same tile, the mark pulled in to 52% of the tile, so its farthest pixel, 177 px from the centre, sits inside the 204.8 px safe circle (the aim is about 176 px, leaving a margin).
- **Favicons:** #FFFFFF tile, 22% corner radius. Each size has its own cut:

  | size | cut | mark size | margins (left, top, right, bottom), measured | clean pixels, predicted |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 16 px | the mark | 10 px | 3, 3, 3, 3 | 81% |
  | 32 px | the mark | 26 px | 4, 3, 4, 3 | 89% |
  | 48 px | the mark | 38 px | 6, 5, 6, 5 | 93% |

  Each cut is hinted before it is drawn. Its box is set on whole pixels; every cut is centred to the pixel. Stems and bars that are straight, or within half a pixel of straight (a serif face's slightly flared stems, the longest straight part of a stem that flows into its bracket, and three quarters of a pixel for a long straight edge), are made straight, and their edges and every curve's outermost point move to whole pixels, shape by shape, so those strokes render as solid pixels rather than half-tones; diagonals and curves stay anti-aliased. Points two shapes share (a common vertex or edge) are welded to one position, so no crack of the ground opens where they meet (verify checks the pixels deep inside the ink for it), and where hinting closes a thin gap between two shapes of one colour, the speck it would leave at the gap's end is filled. A band that holds a stroke keeps at least one pixel however thin, so hinting never deletes an arm or a bar (verify compares every cut with its unhinted outline to prove it); a stem keeps its own width rounded, so equal stems stay equal; edges that nearly meet share a pixel line, so a counter never notches the stem beside it; a counter keeps a pixel only where it separates strokes that face each other. A shape that is mirror-symmetric about its box is hinted on one side and mirrored, and two shapes that mirror each other share one hinting, so a symmetric mark stays symmetric (verify checks the pixels). On a tile, the cut is made 2 px smaller until no ink pixel touches the tile's soft rounded corner. `favicon.svg` carries the 32 and 16 px cuts and shows the 16 px one when drawn under 24 px; `favicon.ico` holds the 16, 32 and 48 px renders.

**Tabs.** The tile #FFFFFF against Chrome's tab colours: white (light, active tab) 1.0:1 (OKLab dE 0.00), #DEE1E6 (light, tab strip) 1.31:1 (OKLab dE 0.09), #202124 (dark, tab strip) 16.1:1 (OKLab dE 0.75), #35363A (dark, active tab) 12.07:1 (OKLab dE 0.67). It blends into white (light, active tab), #DEE1E6 (light, tab strip): on those tabs the icon reads by its ink alone. A tile a step lighter or more saturated would stand out more.

## Website head

Copy the files in `web/` to the site root and put this early in `<head>` (from `web/head-snippet.html`). It is the minimal modern set; obsolete tags (`shortcut icon`, `mask-icon`, `msapplication-*`, extra apple-touch sizes) are deliberately left out.

```html
<!-- Icons: three links and the manifest. Serve the files in web/ at the site root. -->
<link rel="icon" href="/favicon.ico" sizes="32x32">
<link rel="icon" href="/favicon.svg" type="image/svg+xml">
<link rel="apple-touch-icon" href="/apple-touch-icon.png">
<link rel="manifest" href="/site.webmanifest">
<meta name="theme-color" content="#FFFFFF" media="(prefers-color-scheme: light)">
<meta name="theme-color" content="#1B1D1F" media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)">

<!-- Social preview: keep these early in <head>. og:title should be the page title, not the site name. -->
<meta property="og:type" content="website">
<meta property="og:site_name" content="RewardsPro">
<meta property="og:title" content="Cashback your customers spend in your store">
<meta property="og:locale" content="en_US">
<meta property="og:description" content="Paid as Shopify store credit, with VIP tiers. Free.">
<meta property="og:url" content="https://rewardspro.io">
<meta property="og:image" content="https://rewardspro.io/og.jpg">
<meta property="og:image:type" content="image/jpeg">
<meta property="og:image:width" content="1200">
<meta property="og:image:height" content="630">
<meta property="og:image:alt" content="RewardsPro card: the RewardsPro logo mark beside the headline “Cashback your customers spend in your store” on a white background.">
<meta name="twitter:card" content="summary_large_image">
```

`web/site.webmanifest` sets `"display": "standalone"`, `theme_color` #FFFFFF and `background_color` #FFFFFF (the defaults: set "app" in brand.json for "browser", "minimal-ui" or the dark ground). Its `lang` is en.

**Social card** (`web/og.jpg`): light theme, title "Cashback your customers spend in your store" at 48 px in 2 lines (16.91:1), the subtitle at 30 px in Light, the mark at the right, kept inside the centred 1200 x 600 band that X crops to. Every line is measured and set inside its column, clear of the mark and the footer (`og.text_fits` checks the pixels too); lines break at spaces, preferring the end of a sentence, a colon, a dash or a comma. Baseline JPEG, one interleaved scan, sRGB profile. Change the image URL when the card changes: platforms cache by URL. Its `og:image:alt` describes the picture: "RewardsPro card: the RewardsPro logo mark beside the headline “Cashback your customers spend in your store” on a white background."

## Rebuild and verify

Edit `source/brand.json` and run, from inside this folder:

```sh
python3 ~/.kingdom/tools/artcraft-brand/scripts/brandkit.py source/brand.json --out . --rebuild-check
python3 ~/.kingdom/tools/artcraft-brand/scripts/verify.py .        # re-run every check; rewrites verify.json and the section below
```

The build happens in a sibling staging folder and replaces the kit's files only when it finishes, so a failed build leaves this kit as it was. It is deterministic: rebuilding gives byte-identical files, except that the two PDFs can differ, because vectorcraft-cli 0.13.1 can take their creation dates and IDs from the clock. `--rebuild-check` proves it each time by building again from `source/brand.json` in a temp folder and comparing every other file byte for byte.

## What was not checked

- Rendering in real browsers: tab favicons, the favicon.svg size switch and dark-scheme rule, and how a browser chooses between favicon.ico and favicon.svg were not observed.
- Home-screen and launcher masks on real iOS and Android devices; only the 40% safe circle was measured.
- Social previews on live platforms (X, LinkedIn, Slack, iMessage): only file size, dimensions, tags, the JPEG scan layout and a decode by djpeg or sips were checked. Post a test link once before relying on the card.
- Print: PDF colour stays RGB; no CMYK proof, bleed or spot colours.
- Font licensing for outlined wordmarks and embedded OG text.
- APCA contrast is guidance (WCAG 3 drafts); the colour roles use it as a floor, but WCAG 2.2 is the bar that was checked for conformance.
- The design itself: the kit reproduces the mark you supplied; it does not judge originality, trademark clearance or cultural reading.
