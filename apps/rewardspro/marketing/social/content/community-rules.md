# Taking part in communities: rules for RewardsPro

Content maker (agent), 2026-11-07, revised the same day after the verifier's review. **PROPOSAL for the owner. Not legal
advice.** The rules are quoted from each platform's own pages, fetched 2026-11-07 by the market researcher
(`p1-market.md` §2.2, §4; the raw captures are in the session scratchpad's `p1raw/`). The Reddit quotes are the
exception: no capture of them was kept (§4.1). Community rules change, so read them again before taking part.

## 1. Who takes part, and how

- **Only RewardsPro's owner, in his own words, from his own account.** There is no brand account on forums. Agents never post,
  reply, like, follow, vote or message anywhere (STRATEGY §3, §14).
- **Agents give the owner facts, never reply text, for forums.** Shopify Community removes "generic, templated, or
  AI-generated messages", and r/shopify (Rule 10) and r/ecommerce (Rule 8) ban AI content. So nothing in
  `replies.md` is for forums, and agents don't draft forum replies. For open-board help, the owner may use the ledger
  sentences and Shopify's own help pages as his sources.
- **Forum time counts as outreach hours,** not social minutes (STRATEGY §8).
- **The disclosure line** goes first wherever RewardsPro is relevant (§3).

## 2. Shopify Community (community.shopify.com)

### 2.1 The rules, quoted

Source: https://community.shopify.com/guidelines, fetched 2026-11-07, no date shown on the page.

- "App recommendations are allowed only on the Ask and Offer board. This applies to all apps, whether you own them,
  are affiliated with them, or have no connection to them. If a discussion on another board calls for app
  recommendations, direct the member to the Ask and Offer board without naming, linking to, or promoting specific
  apps."
- "Prohibited behavior includes: Recommending or promoting apps outside the Ask and Offer board, even when an app is
  relevant to the question or the member explicitly asks for recommendations. Promoting products, services,
  websites, or blogs outside the Ask and Offer board. Copying and pasting the same content across multiple boards or
  topics. Posting generic, templated, or AI-generated messages. Contacting members privately without mutual consent
  or attempting to move conversations off-platform without first providing meaningful public help. … Adding
  promotional links to third-party blogs, consultancy websites, or other external sites in replies."
- On Ask & Offer: "Explain how the app or service addresses the member's specific needs. Clearly disclose any
  ownership, employment, affiliate, or other commercial relationship. Be transparent about fees or costs. … Replies
  such as 'I can help, contact me here' without a meaningful explanation will be removed. Do not post contact
  information … or solicit contact in replies. Add those details to your Community profile bio instead."
- On the Shopify Apps board: "members and app developers may help troubleshoot apps the member already uses.
  Developers may link to official help desk or support resources when necessary … Troubleshooting replies must not
  recommend installing, switching to, or purchasing an app. AI-generated content is highly discouraged and will be
  removed."
- "Do not impersonate Shopify staff or claim to be a Shopify Partner unless you hold the official Community rank.
  Inappropriate use of Shopify branding, including in avatars, may result in an immediate ban." "If an organization
  uses multiple official accounts, each username must include a distinct name and the organization's name, for
  example, name_org."
- "Private messaging is allowed only with mutual consent and after meaningful public discussion. Unsolicited or
  promotional private messages are prohibited".

Enforcement is real: in an Ask & Offer thread of 2026-10-07 (/t/694136), three posts were "flagged by the community
and temporarily hidden", and the developer who stayed visible opened with "I develop Okaeri Bloom, so this is a
developer answer rather than a merchant testimonial" (`p1-market.md` §2.1).

point them to the Ask & Offer board without naming any app.

### 2.3 Profile

- Username: the owner's own choice. If Cambridge TCG ever runs a second official account, the `name_org` rule applies.
- Bio: his own words, or left as it is. `../profiles.md` §4.3 lists the facts he may mention and offers no paste
  text (it dropped CB on 2026-11-07). Contact details go in the bio, never in replies. Add contact@ only after the delivery
  test.
- Avatar: no Shopify branding of any kind ("immediate ban").
- Never claim a Shopify Partner rank, tier or "Built for Shopify".

### 2.4 Checklist for the owner's one Ask & Offer post (S2)

The owner writes it himself. Agents don't draft it. It should:
1. Open with the disclosure (§3).
2. Say what the app does using only `verified` rows. The likely ones are rp-store-credit, rp-paid-trigger,
   rp-vip-tiers, rp-tier-window, rp-refund-takeback, and rp-widget if verified.
3. State the cost plainly, as the guideline asks: rp-free with rp-merchant-funds, plus sh-sc-fee (Shopify's
   third-party transaction fee for stores created on or after 12 May 2025).
4. Give the limit beside the feature: sh-sc-signin-legacy (no legacy customer accounts).
5. Carry no contact details or "contact me" (bio only), and no link beyond the listing. Whether a listing link is
   allowed on Ask & Offer isn't spelled out in the quoted text [U]; the bio is the safe place.
6. Name no competitor, make no comparison, and carry no numbers that aren't in the ledger.
7. Be posted once, on one board.

## 3. The disclosure line

**PROPOSAL: the owner's own words decide every line in this table.** Each line states a person's relationship to the
product, and no cited source states the owner's ("the owner" is only what these files call him, not a sourced
fact). The maker row, rp-maker ("RewardsPro is made by Cambridge TCG Limited,
London, UK."), names the company, never a person. Its sources are the listing and Companies House company 15680297's
overview page, which names no officer. As read at 12:42 on 2026-11-07, its scope makes one exception, for the owner alone to
approve: the first-person line in his own Community bio (STRATEGY §3.2's CB, "I make RewardsPro…", which
`../profiles.md` §4.3 no longer offers), with the owner's yes recorded as
MISSING. The Ask & Offer lines below make the same kind of statement in posts, which that exception doesn't cover
as written. Before the owner uses one, the claims maker can widen the exception or add an internal row (suggested
`yu-relationship`, channel community) sourced to the Companies House officers and PSC records, stating the exact
relationship. Disclosing it at all is a personal-detail decision for the owner.

| Who and where | Line (draft) | Chars |
|---|---|---|
| The owner, Shopify Community bio | his own words; no paste text (`../profiles.md` §4.3 dropped CB, "I make RewardsPro…", on 2026-11-07; STRATEGY §3.2 still lists it) | — |
| The owner, at the start of any Ask & Offer post or reply | Option A: "Disclosure: I make RewardsPro (Cambridge TCG Limited), so this is a developer's answer, not a merchant's." Option B, if the sourced relationship is ownership: "Disclosure: RewardsPro is made by Cambridge TCG Limited, my company, so this is a developer's answer, not a merchant's." | A 105; B 119 |
| Anyone else at Cambridge TCG, if that ever happens | "Disclosure: I work on RewardsPro at Cambridge TCG Limited, which makes it." | 74 |
| The owner, on his personal LinkedIn or X, if he ever posts about RewardsPro (default: he doesn't, D12) | "Ad · I make RewardsPro, an app for Shopify stores." at the very start | 50 |

**Why the line names the relationship.** The guideline asks to "Clearly disclose any ownership, employment,
affiliate, or other commercial relationship" (community.shopify.com/guidelines, fetched 2026-11-07). "I work on"
reads like employment, so it fits only someone employed on it; for the owner, the line should say what his relationship
actually is, once it is sourced. The personal-profile line comes first, because a "we" part-way through wasn't
enough in the ASA's We Are TALA ruling (22 May 2024: the commercial intent "was not made immediately clear upfront";
CAP 2.1, 2.3; https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/we-are-tala-ltd-g23-1218988-we-are-tala-ltd.html).

## 4. Other places

### 4.1 Reddit: don't post about the app

**These quotes are [P]: the market researcher rendered the sidebars once on 2026-11-07, and they couldn't be
re-checked later that day.** A GET of `reddit.com/r/{shopify,ecommerce,smallbusiness}/about/rules.json` returned
HTTP 403, a headless render returned an empty page (the verifier's check), and the scratchpad's `p1raw/rd_*.json`
files are all the same 189,908-byte block page, so no capture of the rules was kept. Read them again before relying
on them.

**r/shopify** (sidebar rules, rendered 2026-11-07):
- Rule 4: "This is not the place to promote yourself or services to the members here. Violations and attempts to
  circumvent this rule by 'recommending someone' inviting private solicitation via DM or request for DM will result
  in a ban. This also includes 'offers to help' (even if free)…"
- Rule 6: "The sharing of any links, be it in a YouTube description or otherwise is not allowed. Content marketing via
  blog articles will not be allowed. Please avoid links for signup for Shopify as well as for apps and external
  courses."
- Rule 8, "No 'Dev Research' or App Developer posts": "Posts looking for 'pain points' of store owners, beta testing,
  app reviews, app validation ideas, or other posts that are looking for ideas or feedback on apps or app / software
  ideas are not allowed."
- Rule 3 bans guides, how-tos and case studies. Rule 9: "minimum account age requirement is 30 days, and our minimum
  karma requirement is 20 'comment' karma AND 20 'post' karma". Rule 10: "Obvious or suspected AI content is not
  welcome here in any form."

**r/ecommerce:** Rule 1, "No Self-Promotion … (even if free) … This is our most strictly enforced rule"; Rule 2, no
external links; Rule 7, no "Dev Research" posts (no beta testers or app reviews); Rule 8, no AI.

**r/smallbusiness:** Rule 3, "Promote your business in the weekly Promote-your-business thread only". A mention in a
relevant reply is tolerated, but "deliberate abuse of this exception to promote will be recognized and removed".
Rule 2: "As of June 2026 product mentions will be removed from new posts or comments if they appear directly or
indirectly promotional." Rule 5 bans market-research posts.

**So** (STRATEGY §3, skip): no RewardsPro posts, no "would you use this?" research posts, no links. If the owner answers
there as himself, from an account that meets Rule 9, he doesn't mention the app at all. Merchant interviews go
through consent-first routes (STRATEGY §9), never Reddit research threads.

### 4.2 LinkedIn

- **The RewardsPro Page** is the one active feed, posting milestones only (`calendar.md`).
- **Automation is banned** (LinkedIn help a1341387, "Prohibited software and extensions", read 2026-11-07): no "bots
  or other unauthorized automated methods to … send or redirect messages, create, comment on, like, share, or
  re-share posts, or otherwise drive inauthentic engagement"; no scraping tools. Agents must not render or scrape
  LinkedIn pages either (`p1-market.md`, method caveat).
- **Groups:** their rules weren't researched, and each group sets its own, which are usually readable only when
  logged in [U]. They aren't in the channel plan. Default: don't post in groups. If the owner ever wants one, he reads that
  group's rules first, and §5 applies.
- **Comments on other people's posts as the Page:** only where RewardsPro is plainly relevant and adds a fact, never
  as a link drop, and never in a run of near-identical comments.
- **Personal profile:** the "Ad ·" line first (§3). Default: no personal posts (D12).

### 4.3 X and Bluesky: reserve only

They never post (STRATEGY §3). For the record, X's Automation rules ("Updated April 2026",
https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-automation) say: "You may not post duplicative or substantially similar
posts on one account or over multiple accounts you operate"; "sending automated replies to posts based on keyword
searches alone is not permitted"; "You may not send unsolicited Direct Messages in a bulk or automated manner"; "the
deployment or operation of any AI reply bot requires prior written and explicit approval from X". DMs stay closed
on X. Bluesky's rules on promotion weren't researched [U].

## 5. What never to do, anywhere

| Never | Why (source) |
|---|---|
| **Sock puppets:** posing as a merchant, asking "has anyone tried RewardsPro?", or getting friends, staff or family to post or review | CAP 2.3: marketing "must not falsely claim or imply that the marketer is acting as a consumer"; DMCCA 2024 Sch 20 para 13 bans "Submitting, or commissioning another person to submit or write— (a) a fake consumer review" (`p1-market.md` §4.3–4.4); Community `name_org` rule |
| **Incentivised reviews:** anything offered for a review, features held back until a review, or reviews written or "improved" for someone | Shopify App Store requirement 1.3.1: "It is forbidden to offer incentives or withhold features in exchange for reviews, regardless of whether this occurs through the app or external channels." (shopify.dev app-store-requirements, read 2026-11-07); DMCCA Sch 20 para 13; CAP 3.45; consequences include delisting (`ref/be-marketing-RESEARCH.md` §3.4) |
| **Review gating:** asking only happy merchants, or hiding bad reviews | CMA208 (2025-04-04), `ref/be-marketing-RESEARCH.md` §3.1; CAP 3.46 |
| **Accepting "paid installs" or "paid reviews" offers** | the same rules; founders report getting such offers (`p1-market.md` §2.1) |
| **Fake urgency:** deadlines, "spots left", "limited places", countdowns, "last chance" | CAP 3.30 (no false "limited time"; asa.org.uk CAP Code section 03); STRATEGY §4, never-say 12 |
| **Comparative claims without evidence:** "only", "first", "best", "#1", "leading", naming or comparing competitors | CAP 3.32–3.43 ("unqualified superlative claims" count as comparisons with all competitors); Business Protection from Misleading Marketing Regulations 2008, regs 3–4; App Store requirements 4.3.3–4.3.4. Koin and Casa are free too, so "the only free…" is false (`p1-market.md` §1.1) |
| **"Free" on its own:** "free forever", "no fees", "costs nothing", "free trial" | CAP 3.23–3.26; ASA "Use of free" (14 Oct 2026) and the Webloyalty cashback case; Shopify's own fee on store-credit payments (STRATEGY §4, never-say 1–2) |
| **Cold private messages or emails** | PECR: social DMs are electronic mail (ICO key concepts); Community: "Unsolicited or promotional private messages are prohibited"; PPA: "Do not email a Merchant without their prior consent." (ppa.txt:330) |
| **Templated, AI-written or copy-pasted forum posts** | Community guidelines; r/shopify Rule 10; r/ecommerce Rule 8 |
| **Automation:** auto-replies, auto-likes, bulk follows, scraping | LinkedIn a1341387; X Automation rules |
| **Shopify marks as our identity:** "Shopify" in a name, handle, URL slug, UTM value or hashtag; Shopify logos in avatars or our art; "official", "Shopify-approved", Partner tiers | PPA 5.3 (ppa.txt:453); Part C s.3 allows naming Shopify only for compatibility (ppa.txt:718); Community branding ban |
| **Posts on Shopify's behalf, or disparaging Shopify** | PPA §2.1 (ppa.txt:337): "false, misleading or disparaging representations" |
| **Personal details** about the owner or his relationships; links to KINGDOM sites, github.com/cambridgetcg or docs.rewardspro.io; KINGDOM, love, citizen or liturgical words | STRATEGY §4, never-say 14; R1 voice |
| **Numbers** not in the ledger: installs, stores, reviews, credit issued, ratings, uplift | STRATEGY §4, never-say 9; App Store 4.3.3 (no statistics in the listing) |
| **Tax, VAT, accounting or legal advice** in a reply | STRATEGY §4, never-say 16 |

**Before anything goes out, check:**
1. The disclosure comes first.
2. Every fact is a `verified` row, or a Shopify help page with its date.
3. "Free" sits beside "you fund the cashback", with the fee sentence where there is room.
4. There is no link the board forbids.
5. No contact details are in the reply.
6. It isn't a copy of an earlier post.
7. The owner wrote it.
