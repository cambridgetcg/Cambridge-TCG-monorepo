Subprocessors

Who handles shopper data for RewardsPro.

When RewardsPro runs a store’s loyalty programme, we process that store’s shopper data as its processor, under the data-processing terms. These are the providers that handle that data for us.

Last updated 3 November 2026

Subprocessors

Provider What it does for RewardsPro Where it handles data
Supabase Hosts the RewardsPro database, which holds all the shopper data the app keeps. Database in London, UK. Supabase’s staff may access it from other countries, under Supabase’s data-processing terms.
Vercel Runs the RewardsPro application, which processes shopper data from Shopify, and keeps its application logs. Functions in London, UK. Vercel is a US company, and its network carries requests worldwide. Its logs and analytics may be stored in the United States and other countries where Vercel or its providers operate.
Sentry Error and performance monitoring. Events can include shop and customer identifiers; tokens, cookies and email addresses are removed first. It does not record sessions. Sentry’s EU region (Germany)
Twilio SendGrid Delivers RewardsPro’s emails to shoppers (welcome, tier and membership emails, and the campaigns a merchant sends), and reports delivery events such as bounces and unsubscribes. United States

Other providers

These support our own website, email and help tools. The RewardsPro app doesn’t send them shopper data, but a message sent to us can contain it.

Provider What it does for RewardsPro Where it handles data
Cloudflare Hosts the rewardspro.io website and runs its DNS and network security. Cloudflare’s global network
GitBook Answers merchant staff’s help questions through its documentation assistant, when they use it. [Yu: confirm GitBook’s data location]
Google Workspace Hosts the contact@rewardspro.io mailbox, including privacy and support requests. Google’s data centres worldwide, including the United States

Not our subprocessors

Shopify is your commerce platform and the source of the data; Shopify’s own terms cover your relationship with it. Services you connect yourself, such as Klaviyo, receive data on your instruction and are your providers, not ours.

Changes to this list

Before we add or replace a subprocessor that handles shopper data, we update this page and tell merchants by email, as the data-processing terms describe. Questions go to contact@rewardspro.io.
