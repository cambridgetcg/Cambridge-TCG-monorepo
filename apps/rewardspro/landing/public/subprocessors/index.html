Subprocessors

The providers that handle personal data for RewardsPro: what each does for it, what data reaches it, and where.

List dated 10 November 2026

For a shop's customers' data

RewardsPro processes the data about a shop's customers for the shop, as our privacy policy explains. These providers handle that data for us.

Provider What it does for RewardsPro What data reaches it Where
Supabase Hosts the app's database. Every record the app keeps: shops and their settings, customers' Shopify IDs and tiers, orders, refunds, cashback, and Shopify's data requests. Until we delete them, it also holds the previous version's records, which can include customers' names and contact details. London, UK
Vercel Runs the app and its scheduled jobs, and keeps a log of each request to it for 1 day. Everything the app reads from Shopify and writes to its database passes through it. Its request logs include the Shopify customer ID on a signed-in customer's widget request, the shop's myshopify.com address, the search words staff type on the Customers page, and the staff member's short-lived Shopify sign-in token. Its logs also hold the app's own log lines, which can name the shop and Shopify's order and refund IDs. The app runs in London, UK
Sentry Receives the app's error reports. The shop's myshopify.com address and technical details of the error, which can include Shopify's order and refund IDs, RewardsPro's internal record IDs, the app's recent log lines, and the request's web address and headers, such as the browser type. Tokens, cookies, IP addresses, query strings, request bodies and email addresses are removed first. Sentry's hosted service. Write to us for the region.

For our website and our email

The app sends these providers no data. A message someone sends us can still mention a shop or its customers.

Provider What it does for RewardsPro What data reaches it Where
Cloudflare Hosts rewardspro.io and runs its DNS. Visitors' network details, such as IP addresses. The site sets no cookies. Cloudflare's global network
Google Workspace Hosts the contact@rewardspro.io mailbox. The emails people send us, and our replies. Google's data centres

Not on this list

Shopify is the platform a shop runs on and where the data comes from: it sends the app the shop's orders, refunds and privacy requests, holds the customers' store credit, and sends the store-credit email when a shop turns that on. Shopify's own terms and privacy policy cover it.

The app sends no email and uses no other provider. When this list changes, we update this page and its date. Questions go to contact@rewardspro.io.
