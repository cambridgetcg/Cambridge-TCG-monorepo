RewardsPro help

As of 9 November 2026.

What is RewardsPro?

RewardsPro, made by Cambridge TCG Limited in London, UK, is an app for Shopify stores to reward loyal customers. RewardsPro pays cashback as Shopify store credit and runs VIP tiers by spend; it offers no other reward types. The cashback is store credit in your own shop, so you fund the cashback you give.

What should you know before you start?

Three limits:

  • Customers can spend store credit at checkout when signed in with Shopify's new customer accounts or with Shop Pay; it isn't available for legacy customer accounts.
  • Shopify doesn't let store credit pay subscription renewals.
  • Each tier has one cashback rate, with no setting to exclude products, collections or sales channels.

Which Shopify settings does your store need?

For customers to spend their credit, your store needs store credit turned on, new customer accounts and visible sign-in links. RewardsPro's Home warns you when Shopify shows one of these still needs doing, with a banner that starts "Buyers can't spend their store credit yet". When Shopify doesn't fully answer, Home shows "Store credit: Shopify didn't say" and no banner.

Shopify's rule

Shopify's help page says to turn on the Store credit toggle in the Customer accounts section, and to activate the Apply store credit option in the Payments section of the checkout.

Source: Shopify Help Center, "Store credit", as read on 7 November 2026.

How do you set up RewardsPro?

A new store starts with cashback switched off and no tiers. RewardsPro's setup card has three steps: set a base cashback rate, switch cashback on, and turn on the widget in your theme editor. On Home, the card reads:

  1. Your base cashback rate: a whole percent from 0 to 100. Saving it creates your lowest tier, Everyone, which starts at a spend of 0.
  2. Switch cashback on. The button works once the base rate is saved.
  3. Turn on the widget in your theme, which opens your theme editor.

When you first open RewardsPro, it starts bringing in your paid orders from up to 12 months before install, so loyal customers can start in the tier their past spend reaches. Past orders earn no cashback. The 12 months need Shopify's access to your full order history; without it, the import reaches back 60 days. If your past orders can't be brought in, Home says so, and customers start in the lowest tier.

How can you try it on your own store?

Once cashback is on, create a draft order for a customer and mark it as paid. The cashback is real store credit on that customer's account in your shop. Refunding the order takes back its share of the cashback. Orders with no customer attached, and Shopify's test orders, earn nothing.

How do you turn on the widget?

Shopify adds the widget to your theme switched off, as an app embed. The setup card's link, Turn on the widget in your theme, opens your theme editor with the widget switched on under App embeds. Save the theme to keep the change. In the theme editor the widget is called RewardsPro Widget. If it is off, Home shows "The rewards widget is off in your theme." with a link to the theme editor.

Its settings:

  • Guest message, Guest button text and Guest button link replace the sign-in prompt guests see.
  • Colours: Light or Dark.
  • Accent fills the progress bar; text never uses it.

What do customers see?

The widget is a Rewards button at the bottom right of the page that opens a panel. While cashback is on, a widget you turn on in your theme editor shows signed-in customers their tier and cashback rate, their store credit in the currency they are shopping in when Shopify returns it, and how far they are from the next tier when there is one they can reach. Guests see your base rate and a sign-in prompt.

  • When Shopify doesn't return the balance, the panel reads "Your store credit shows at checkout".
  • A customer in your top tier sees "You are on the top tier".
  • With cashback off, guests see nothing, and signed-in customers see their tier and store credit without a rate.
  • The widget's text is in English.

What if signed-in customers don't see the widget?

The signed-in widget needs RewardsPro's app proxy path, /apps/rewardspro. If another app already holds that path on your store, Shopify gives RewardsPro a different one, such as /apps/rewardspro-1, and the signed-in widget stays hidden while Home still says the widget is on. If that happens, email contact@rewardspro.io and we'll help.

How much cashback does an order earn?

Cashback is paid on the product price after discounts, as the order stood when it was paid, less any part of the order paid with store credit or gift cards. Shipping, and gift cards bought as products, earn nothing. Duties and tips earn nothing either.

The rate is the one for the tier the customer held before the order counted. So the order that takes a customer into a higher tier earns at their old rate, and their next order earns at the new one.

Which orders earn nothing?

  • Orders with no customer attached.
  • Shopify's test orders, and cancelled orders.
  • Orders placed before you installed RewardsPro, even if they are paid later.
  • Past orders RewardsPro brings in from before install.
  • Orders while cashback is off. They still count towards tiers.
  • Orders too large for RewardsPro to read: more than 2,500 lines, 200 or more payment transactions, or 20 or more refunds.

Cashback is rounded once, half up, to the currency's smallest unit, such as the penny.

When is the store credit added?

For an order that earns cashback, payment is what triggers it; RewardsPro doesn't wait for the order to be fulfilled. If Shopify refuses the credit for now, RewardsPro tries again for up to 24 hours. A refusal that can't change, or one still failing after 24 hours, marks the order "Needs a look", and Home shows how many orders need a look.

How do customers spend their store credit?

Shopify's rule

  • Customers can spend store credit at checkout when signed in with Shopify's new customer accounts or with Shop Pay; it isn't available for legacy customer accounts.
  • Shopify applies the full store-credit balance at checkout; customers can't choose part of it.
  • At checkout, Shopify shows only the store-credit balance in the checkout's currency.
  • Store credit can be spent on the online store, POS and Shop, but not on draft or edited orders.

Source: Shopify Help Center, "Store credit", as read on 7 November 2026.

On stores with new customer accounts, the widget tells signed-in customers: "Sign in and choose Apply store credit at checkout".

What about customers who pay in another currency?

RewardsPro gives cashback in the currency the customer paid in. So credit from an order paid in US dollars shows at a checkout in US dollars, and not at a checkout in pounds.

Does store credit expire?

RewardsPro sets no expiry date on the credit it gives.

How do VIP tiers work?

Set tiers by spend, each with its own cashback rate. Loyal customers move between tiers as their spend changes, unless you lock them to a tier. Each tier has a name, a minimum spend in whole amounts of your shop's currency, and a cashback rate in whole percent from 0 to 100. A new customer starts in your lowest tier, which begins at a spend of 0.

What counts as spend?

A customer's spend is the product price after discounts, less any products refunded, in your shop's currency. Test and cancelled orders don't count. Tiers count a loyal customer's spend over the last 12 months or over all time, from the orders RewardsPro has recorded: those since install, and past orders from up to 12 months before it. You choose one for the whole store under Spend counted over: Last 12 months, the default, or All time.

When do customers move?

A customer's tier is updated after each paid order, refund and cancellation, and in a nightly pass. There is no grace period: when a customer's counted spend falls below their tier's minimum, they move down. After you add a tier, change a minimum or change the window, customers move to their new tiers; in a large store some move overnight. Renaming a tier or changing its rate moves no one.

Can you keep a customer in a tier?

You can lock a customer to a tier. A locked customer keeps that tier and its rate whatever they spend, until you choose Return to automatic. The Lock to a tier control is on a customer's page, once RewardsPro has recorded an order of theirs. Deleting a tier returns customers locked to it to automatic.

What happens to cashback when you refund an order?

When items are refunded, RewardsPro takes back their share of the cashback, up to the store credit the customer still holds in the order's currency. A refund of shipping alone takes nothing back.

  • The share follows the product value: refunding half of an order's product value takes back half its cashback, and the refund that completes the order takes back what is left.
  • If the credit hasn't been sent yet, the refund reduces it before it is sent.
  • If the customer has already spent the credit, RewardsPro takes what is left and records the rest; it doesn't chase it.
  • The take-back comes from the customer's store-credit balance in that currency, whatever gave them the credit.
  • Cancelling an order moves no money by itself; the refund does.

Two limits:

  • On a store whose prices exclude tax, a refund of money alone, with no items, takes back a little more than its share.
  • If an order is edited after payment to add items, refunding those items can take back more than their share, up to all of the order's cashback.

What does it cost you?

RewardsPro is free for every store that installs it, and no paid plans are offered.

The cashback is store credit in your own shop, so you fund the cashback you give. The cost lands when customers spend the credit at checkout.

Shopify's rule

For Shopify stores created on or after 12 May 2025, Shopify charges third-party transaction fees on the part of an order paid with store credit. The rate depends on your Shopify plan.

Sources: Shopify Help Center, "Store credit" and "Third-party transaction fees", as read on 7 November 2026.

Does RewardsPro send emails, and what data does it keep?

RewardsPro sends no email. One setting, off by default, asks Shopify to send its own store-credit email. It is Email buyers when they earn store credit, under Settings. That email is Shopify's template, which you can edit in Shopify's customer notifications.

What happens when a customer asks for their data?

When a customer asks you for their data, Shopify sends the request to the apps on your store. RewardsPro shows it inside the app, not by email: a banner on Home, and a list under Settings > Customer data requests. The banner asks you to download the report by a date 30 days after the request arrived. The report can be downloaded for 45 days from the request; after that it can't. It is built when you download it, from what RewardsPro holds about that customer. RewardsPro stores no customer names or email addresses; the app reads names from Shopify when it shows them.

Privacy policy

What happens when you uninstall?

When you uninstall, RewardsPro stops working for your store: it records no more orders and sends no more credit. Shopify removes the widget from your theme. Store credit your customers already have stays on their accounts in Shopify.

48 hours after you uninstall, Shopify asks RewardsPro to erase your store's data, and RewardsPro erases it. If you reinstall before then, your data stays and RewardsPro carries on. If Shopify's request never arrives, a job that runs each night erases the data once 30 days have passed since you uninstalled.
