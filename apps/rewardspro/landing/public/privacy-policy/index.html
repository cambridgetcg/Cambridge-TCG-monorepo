Who we are

For anything on this page, write to us at contact@rewardspro.io.

Who decides about the data

A shop that installs RewardsPro uses it to give its customers cashback and tiers. For the data about the shop's customers, the shop decides how it is used, and we process it for the shop, on its instructions. For how a shop uses your data, read that shop's privacy notice.

For the shop's own account with us, and for people who write to us or visit this website, Cambridge TCG Limited decides how the data is used.

What the app keeps about a shop's customers

RewardsPro reads the shop's orders from Shopify and keeps what it needs to work out cashback and tiers:

  • the customer's Shopify customer ID;
  • their tier, the date they reached it, and any tier the shop has set for them by hand;
  • for each paid order: Shopify's order ID and order number (such as #1001), its currency and amounts, any store credit or gift card used on it, the dates it was placed, paid or cancelled, and whether it was a test order;
  • for each refund: Shopify's refund ID, the amount and the date;
  • the cashback given and taken back, with the tier and rate used, and the outcome of each store-credit change sent to Shopify;
  • Shopify's data requests about the customer: the customer ID, the orders the request names, and its dates.

RewardsPro's database holds no customer names, email addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses. When a page in the app shows a customer's name, the app reads it from Shopify at that moment and doesn't keep it. The shop's staff can download a customer list as a spreadsheet, with each customer's name, tier, spend counted and cashback given; RewardsPro makes that file when it is downloaded and keeps no copy. Orders that Shopify links to no customer are not recorded.

When a shop first opens RewardsPro, the app reads the shop's orders from the 12 months before installation (the last 60 days, if Shopify hasn't given the app access to older orders) and keeps the paid ones, to place customers in tiers. Those past orders don't earn cashback.

What the app does with it

  • It adds cashback to a customer's store credit in Shopify when their order is paid, and takes it back when the order is refunded.
  • It works out each customer's tier from their spend, by the rules the shop sets.
  • It shows a signed-in customer their tier and store-credit balance in the shop's storefront widget.
  • If the shop turns the setting on, it asks Shopify to email a customer when they earn store credit. Shopify sends that email. RewardsPro sends no email.
  • It answers Shopify's privacy requests, as described under How long it is kept, and erasure.

The app sends no shop or customer data to an AI service, and we don't use that data to train AI models. We don't sell personal data.

The storefront widget

The widget sets no cookies and stores nothing in the customer's browser. For a signed-in customer, it asks RewardsPro for their tier and store-credit balance through the shop's own website address; Shopify adds the shop and the customer's Shopify customer ID to that request and signs it. The balance is read from Shopify each time and not kept. For a guest, the widget sends no request to RewardsPro.

What we keep about a shop and its staff

  • The shop's name, its myshopify.com address, its currency and its time zone, read from Shopify.
  • The shop's RewardsPro settings and tiers, and the dates it installed, opened and uninstalled the app.
  • Shopify's access tokens for the shop, stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM).

The app keeps no names or email addresses of a shop's staff. We use the shop's data because it is needed to provide the app the shop installed, and because it is in our legitimate interest to keep RewardsPro secure and working.

People who write to us, and this website

If you email contact@rewardspro.io, we keep your message and your email address in that mailbox, which Google Workspace hosts, so that we can answer you and keep a record of what was said. We keep emails for as long as we need them for that. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering the people who write to us.

Cloudflare hosts rewardspro.io. Its pages set no cookies and load no analytics or advertising scripts. Cloudflare handles each visit's network details, such as the IP address, to serve and protect the site.

Who handles the data

Our subprocessors page lists each provider, what it does for RewardsPro, what data reaches it and where. In short:

  • Supabase hosts the app's database, in London.
  • Vercel runs the app, in London, and keeps a log of each request to it for 1 day.
  • Sentry receives the app's error reports.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website and its domain's DNS.
  • Google Workspace hosts the contact@rewardspro.io mailbox.

For that day, Vercel's request logs hold the Shopify customer ID that Shopify adds to a signed-in customer's widget request, the shop's myshopify.com address, the search words staff type on the app's Customers page, and the short-lived Shopify sign-in token of the staff member using the app.

When something goes wrong, the app sends Sentry an error report. A report can carry the shop's myshopify.com address and technical details of the error, including RewardsPro's internal record IDs. Before a report is sent, the app removes tokens, cookies, query strings, request bodies and email addresses from it. RewardsPro sends Sentry no performance traces and runs no session recording.

Shopify is the platform the shop runs on and where the data comes from. It holds the customers' store credit and sends the store-credit email when a shop turns it on. For Shopify's own handling of data, read Shopify's privacy policy.

How long it is kept, and erasure

While a shop has RewardsPro installed, the app keeps its records, with no fixed end date.

When a shop uninstalls

RewardsPro stops working for the shop and deletes its Shopify access tokens at once. Its other records stay for now, so a shop that reinstalls carries on where it left off. About 48 hours after the uninstall, Shopify asks the app to erase the shop's data, and the app then erases the shop's records, its customers' records included, in one step. If that request doesn't succeed, a nightly job erases the records once 30 days have passed since the uninstall. If the shop has reinstalled RewardsPro by then, nothing is erased. Store credit already given stays in the customers' Shopify accounts, because Shopify holds it.

When a customer asks for their data

Shopify passes the request to RewardsPro, and it shows in the app, on Home and under Settings, Customer data requests, for the shop to download and answer. It is due 30 days after it arrives, and the report can be downloaded for 45 days. RewardsPro builds the report from its records when it is downloaded and keeps no copy. The request's own record stays until the customer's or the shop's records are erased.

When a customer asks for their data to be erased

Shopify passes the request to RewardsPro. The app removes the customer's Shopify customer ID from its records, removes any tier the shop set for them by hand, deletes Shopify's data requests about them, and stops any store-credit change still waiting to be sent for them. The amounts of their past orders and cashback stay in the shop's records, with Shopify's order and transaction numbers, but without the Shopify customer ID.

Logs, error reports and backups

Vercel's request logs are kept for 1 day. Error reports stay in Sentry until Sentry deletes them under the retention period of our account. Erased records can remain in our database host's backups until those backups expire.

The previous version's records

RewardsPro was rebuilt, and the previous version of the app stopped running on 6 November 2026. Its records stay, unused, in a separate part of the same database in London until we delete them. They can include the names, email addresses, phone numbers and birthdays of customers of shops that used the previous version. Shopify's privacy requests now reach only the current app's records, so if you want the previous version's records about you or your shop copied or erased before then, write to contact@rewardspro.io.

Your rights

Under UK data-protection law you can ask for a copy of your personal data, and ask for it to be corrected or erased, or for its use to be restricted or stopped. If you are a customer of a shop that uses RewardsPro, the shop decides about your data, so ask the shop first; Shopify passes access and erasure requests on to RewardsPro, as described above. You can also write to us at contact@rewardspro.io, and we will pass your request to the shop or help it answer. We normally answer within one month, as UK law requires, subject to the extensions and exemptions the law allows.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, write to us first if you can. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Security and transfers

The app checks Shopify's signature on every webhook and every widget request, stores Shopify's access tokens encrypted, and connects to its database over TLS checked against Supabase's root certificate. Its tables sit in their own schema, under their own database role. To report a suspected data incident, write to contact@rewardspro.io.

The app's database and the app itself run in London. Vercel, Sentry, Cloudflare and Google can handle data outside the UK, including in the United States. Write to us for the safeguards that apply to a particular provider.

When RewardsPro changes what it keeps or who handles it, we update this page and the date at the top.
