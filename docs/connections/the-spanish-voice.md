THE SPANISH VOICE — doctrine summary (binding; full charter in the-spanish-voice source).

REGISTER. Warm pan-Hispanic tuteo: the museum host who also keeps a small card shop, speaking to someone already inside. No voseo, no vosotros (plural address is ustedes with tú-warmth in the verbs), no country-marking words — identical from Madrid to Mexico City to Buenos Aires. Usted is the receipt voice, banned from the shop floor; only legal pages use impersonal-se/formal. Pro-drop carries the intimacy: verbs conjugate tú and nosotros, each explicit pronoun must be load-bearing. The house is 'esta casa' (humility register: 'la tienda' — guard the sentence 'Somos una tienda de cartas, no académicos.'). Banned: plataforma, empresa, usuario, cliente. The visitor is 'coleccionista' or 'quien llega'. Gender neutrality by rewording (quien, cada cual, agreement with abstractions: 'bienvenida toda existencia'), never todxs/todes/tod@s. Headings are sentence-case noun-phrase wall labels, no final period.

KEY GLOSSARY. Mercado; el mercado entre coleccionistas; Precios; el cuaderno de precios (guía de precios only as first-mention findability gloss); precio de referencia; Jugar; Cultura; Plaza (Community); comprar y vender / de mano en mano (trade); intercambio, cambiar carta por carta (swap); subasta; escrow = 'queda guardada aquí mientras tanto' in prose, la custodia only in methodology/legal; cuidar / velar por (protect); Para empezar; Lo que cuesta (fees); Sobre esta casa; Poner una carta a la venta; catálogo (never base de datos); el sobre (the pack, not 'pull'); benedictions: 'Cada carta es una viñeta en la historia de alguien.' / 'Las cartas vienen de alguna parte. Estas salas recuerdan de dónde.' The radical welcome lives in the concessive subjunctive ('sea biológica o no, venga de donde venga') — precision, never incense.

PUNCTUATION LAW. Exclamation marks banned absolutely, both ¡ and !; no caps/emoji substitutes. Prefer indirect interrogatives (qué es un TCG); a surviving true question wears both ¿…?; a bare trailing ? is a banned anglicism. Comillas «» outer, "" inner. Raya is native: paired for incidentals (—inciso—), single end-raya at most once per screen; default conversion of the EN dash is full stop + a smaller sentence. Figures mirror the data layer verbatim: £12.50, 1,234, 10–13%, 0% tight against the sign. Single-character …, never in benedictions. →, ✦, · pass through. CJK marks hang unchanged beside Spanish titles; routes and code literals are substrate.

DONTS. Receipt usted; regional drift; ad-copy hype (freeness stated as quiet fact, 'gratis' lowercase only); hidden exclamation energy; anglicism flood; possessive spray (article over tu); baroque subordination — the short sentence is Spanish's 間; corporate nosotros; sect vocabulary; -ito diminutives; stacked -mente; Spanish must not run longer than the EN (cut, don't compress). Signature smile: colon-then-answer — 'Comisión: ninguna.'
