THE TRADITIONAL CHINESE VOICE (zh-Hant) — binding charter, synthesized 2026-07-29.

REGISTER: Warm standard written Chinese with a Cantonese heart — a Hong Kong shopkeeper who is also a museum host, writing (never transcribing speech) to someone already inside the room. No dialect particles in body copy (嘅/咗/係/唔 banned); the warmth lives in vocabulary: 舖, 街坊, 不用錢, 照直說, 誰人, 由這裡開始. Address is 你, only and sparingly — 您/閣下 banned outright; default to zero subject. The visitor is never 顧客/客戶; collectors are 藏家, anyone at the door 來的人. Self-name: omission first, then 小店; 本店/本公司/本平台/敝店 banned. Headings and chips are bare nouns in wall-label register (行情、新到、文化) — no 的, no verb, no stop. Benedictions: one line, plain clauses, clean 。, no particles (呢/喔/啊), nothing after. Legal pages may be formal — honesty includes not dressing law as poetry.

GLOSSARY (one truth): 市集 market; 行情 prices; 行情簿 price guide; 參考價 reference price; 玩 play; 街坊 community; 藏家 collector; 買賣 trade (交易 only legal/technical); 交換 swap; 以卡易卡 card-for-card; 拍賣 auction; 居中保管 escrow; 看顧 protect; 上架 list; 指南 guides; 由這裡開始 start here; 手續費的事 fees (page truth: 手續費，沒有。); 資料目錄 data directory; 自報身份 identify; 淺色/深色 themes; 用戶端 client; 私隱 privacy; 紀錄 the noun, 記錄 the verb; {n}張 card counts. "agent" stays Latin — a named gap. 細聲講大聲笑 stands bare: no quotes, no jyutping, no gloss — the house's handwriting come home. Wing marks 墨與留白, 浮世工房, 繪師們, 賭局與命運, 抽與靜, 機率，照直說。, 唱和, 鄰家畫廊 are offers until each wing hangs its own; 文化大交流/文化/新展 pass through.

PUNCTUATION: Full-width ，。；：「」（）？ throughout Chinese text; 、 for enumeration, ， for breath — never flatten the distinction. Exclamation marks banned in any width. Prefer statements over questions (第一次來，也不要緊。). 《》 for published works. Em-dash converts to ， or a sentence split; —— only when the held breath is the point, once per page; meta-title separator is ｜. Ellipsis is ……, never in benedictions. Latin/digits half-width always (OP01-001, /market, 0%, £15, 1,234張), no hand-typed spacing beside Latin runs. → and ✦ survive; kicker interpunct is ・. No italics on CJK.

DONTS: receipt voice (您/敬/本-); CS-template warmth; ad hype (立即/限時/優惠); stacked 的-clauses from English syntax; dialect transcription; Sinosphere vocabulary drift (pick the HK written word once: 資訊, 資料, 影片); incense on the welcome (眾生/萬物/靈魂 — radicalism lives in …也好/無論…都 grammar); four-character idiom shop-signs; pronoun spray; metronome sentences; particle-tailed benedictions; interrogative creep. Every EN hedge, number, and disclaimer survives — a sentence more certain than its source is a mistranslation. The page should be shorter than the English: cut, don't compress; the 留白 does the rest.
