# The Simplified Chinese voice — 简体中文的声音

Binding voice charter for every The Simplified Chinese voice string on the site,
written by its own charter fleet (voice director → three transcreators →
refuter pairs → synthesizer) on 2026-07-29, following the method of
[the-japanese-voice.md](./the-japanese-voice.md). **Do not add or edit a
string in this voice without reading it.** Strings live co-located with
their English (Poly records, `zh-Hans` seats), picked via `tx()` in
`lib/i18n.ts`.

House laws that hold here as everywhere: no exclamation marks; every
hedge, number and limit survives; the radical welcome stays radical
without incense; benedictions land as afterglow.

---

Simplified Chinese (zh-Hans) is a SIBLING voice, written independently — never a script conversion of Traditional, never a calque of the Japanese precedent.

REGISTER. Warm plain Mandarin of an independent shopkeeper. Address is 你, never 您. Zero-subject sentences are the default; when the house names itself: 这家店 (default), 小店 (humility), 我们 only when load-bearing. Banned self-names: 本平台/我司/本公司/本站/官方. The visitor is never 顾客/客户/亲/用户 (用户 legal-technical only); roles are 卡友 and 来逛的人. Headings and wing marks are bare nouns of 2–6 characters, no 句号. Legal pages may be formally written. Tone test for every string: say it across the counter — if it could be an app notification, ticket macro, or Taobao banner, rewrite it.

GLOSSARY. 集市 market; 行情 prices, 行情册 price guide; 参考价 reference price; 玩 play; 广场 community; 卡友 collector; 收与出 trade; 换卡/以卡换卡 swap; 上架 list (never JA 出品); 担保交易/替双方保管 escrow; 保障 label, 护着 prose (这家店护着人，不管着人); 手续费 fees (never 手数料); 集换式卡牌 TCG; 卡牌数据名录 data directory (数据, never 资料); 智能体 agent (never 代理); 找 find (搜索 aria-only); 关于这家店 / 这家店的来历; 新到 new; 基质 substrate; 数的语言 math toggle; 渡口 on-ramp. Culture wing titles (笔墨与留白, 浮世工坊, 画师们, 命运的对局, 手气与静气, 概率，照实说。, 唱和, 隔壁的画廊) are offers pending house adoption — the wing owns its mark.

PUNCTUATION. Full-width ，。：；？, every sentence ends 。; 顿号 、 for parallel items; mainland quotes “ ”, never 「」; 《》 for works; no exclamation marks at all — nor smuggled cheer (~, emoji, 哦/啦); —— at most once per screen; ellipsis ……, never in benedictions; Latin/digits half-width, routes/SKUs/API names verbatim; kicker separator 间隔号 · (never ・); → and ✦ pass through; no hand-typed spaces between scripts in prose.

DONTS. 电商腔 (立即购买/限时/秒杀/包邮); 客服腔 (您/亲/感谢您的理解); Taiwan/HK leaks (「」, 资讯, 网路, 透过, 资料-for-data); JA calques (出品/手数料/相场/复数-for-plural); 官话PPT腔 (赋能/打造/生态/闭环); idiom varnish; gacha slang (爆率/欧皇); sect tone on the welcome (灵魂/能量场/缘分/家人们) — radical scope lives in 无论……都 and 是……，或不是 grammar; pronoun fog; stacked 的-chains; uniform written drone. Benedictions: one line, ends 。, nothing after. Every EN hedge, number, and disclaimer survives. Cambridge TCG stays Latin everywhere; 細聲講大聲笑 keeps its original Cantonese glyphs with a one-line gloss (粤语：小声说话，大声笑). The page should be shorter than the English — cut, don't compress; 留白 is the voice's own name for its rhythm.
